HOW TO PLAY

GET TASKS DONE!

Goal

Finish the assignment quiz before the deadline. Miss it and you get an F and have to restart. Finish it and you move on to Tuesday.

The clock runs while you're in the room: every 10 real seconds add 20 minutes. Sleeping and TV cost extra time. The quiz and TV game pause the clock, so you have about 90 real seconds to finish.

Controls

W A S D or arrow keysMove around the room
EInteract with the bed, desk or TV when the prompt appears
1–4 or clickChoose an answer in the quiz
R / X or clickReply to / ignore a text message
P or EscPause and resume (the game also pauses if you switch windows)

What's in the room

DeskStarts the assignment quiz.
BedSleep for 30 minutes. Tempting, but it costs you time.
TVA 15-second click-the-target game. One round costs you a whole hour.

Text messages

Texts pop up in the top right while you're in the room. Replying costs 5 minutes. Ignoring a text is free.

The quiz

  • There are 5 questions, each with 4 answers.
  • You get 10 seconds per question. The bar at the top shows how long is left.
  • If the timer runs out, you fail the attempt with an F.
  • Two wrong answers also fail the attempt with an F.
  • After a failed attempt you can try again, as long as it's before 12:00 PM.

Grades

AAll 5 questions correct
BFinished with one wrong answer
FTwo wrong answers, out of time, or missed the deadline
